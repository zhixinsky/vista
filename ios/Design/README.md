# CarVista visual identity

Artwork generated with the built-in imagegen tool on 2026-11-14. Original:
`app-icon-source.png` (1254×1254 RGB). `prepare-assets.ps1` performs only size and
RGB-format conversion into the iPhone/iPad icon catalog and the in-app brand mark.
No pre-rounded outer corners or alpha channel; iOS applies its own icon mask.

Prompt:

Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE production-ready iOS app icon for CarVista, an app to customize CarPlay wallpapers. Square 1024x1024 full-bleed opaque image. A distinctive minimal car infotainment display as the single central mark: softly rounded wide screen, silver-white refined rim, inside a vivid teal and turquoise landscape with an elegant winding road flowing toward a small warm ivory sun at the horizon. Deep midnight petrol background, subtle dimensional shading and soft light, crisp geometric silhouette, premium restrained modern Apple-platform visual craft. Mark centered occupying about 70 percent width, generously legible at small size. No car manufacturer logos, no letters, no words, no watermark, no external mockup, no device frame around the whole icon, no rounded outer corners: the artwork fills the entire square, iOS applies its own mask. Keep detail very simple, beautiful contrast, polished and purposeful.
